# Product vision

## Positioning

Traffic Lab is a traffic-planning puzzle and sandbox, not a city-management simulator. Roads and intersections are the primary objects; the simulation is deterministic and explainable.

## North-star loop

1. Read a traffic brief.
2. Draw or revise roads.
3. Run the demand.
4. Inspect where and why queues form.
5. Change the network.
6. Rerun the same scenario and compare the result.

## First milestone

Desktop keyboard and mouse, small fixed maps, cars only, fixed origin/destination demand, three road types, two intersection controls, congestion overlays, transparent metrics, and short challenge scenarios.

See [core-loop.md](core-loop.md) for the required player loop and [non-goals.md](non-goals.md) for the first-milestone boundary.

## Explicit non-goals for the first milestone

Buildings, zoning, population, economy, pedestrians, public transit, rail, aircraft, multiplayer, complex 3D assets, realistic collision physics, and procedural open-world generation.
